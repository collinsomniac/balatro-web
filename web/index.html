Balatro web

Plays your own copy of Balatro in the browser. Nothing from the game is hosted here — you pick the file once, it's patched on this device and kept in this browser's storage.

Steam: steamapps/common/Balatro/Balatro.exe. AirDrop it to Files, then pick it here.

For true full-screen: Share → Add to Home Screen, then launch from the icon and turn your phone sideways.
